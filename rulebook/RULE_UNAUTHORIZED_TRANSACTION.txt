Reason Code: Unauthorized Transaction

This reason code applies when a cardholder claims they did not authorize the transaction (potential card fraud/theft).

REQUIRED EVIDENCE TO CONTEST:
- Confirmation that a valid authentication step occurred at the time of purchase (e.g. OTP/3D Secure verification passed). This is the primary evidence for this reason code, since a successfully completed authentication step strongly indicates the legitimate cardholder approved the transaction.
- Device and IP information consistent with the cardholder's prior purchase history, where available.
- Billing address match with the shipping address, as a secondary signal of legitimacy.

EVIDENCE THAT IS NOT SUFFICIENT ALONE:
- A successful payment capture alone does not prove authorization; payment processing can succeed even on a stolen card if no additional authentication step was performed.
- Delivery confirmation is not directly relevant evidence for this reason code; a fraudulent transaction can still be delivered successfully.

IF AUTHENTICATION CANNOT BE CONFIRMED:
Treat as high fraud risk. Without a confirmed OTP/3DS step, the transaction should generally not be contested.

RESPONSE FORMAT:
State clearly whether OTP/3D Secure authentication was completed, and reference any matching device/IP or address history. Do not rely on delivery or payment-capture evidence as the primary argument.
